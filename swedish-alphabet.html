Det Svenska AlfabetetThe Swedish Alphabet

📚 Explore Swedish: Swedish Basics First Conversation At the Restaurant At the Airport Typical Day Swedish Hub →

The Swedish alphabet has 29 letters: the 26 you know from English, plus Å, Ä and Ö at the end. Nine of them are vowels (a, e, i, o, u, y, å, ä, ö). The table below gives each letter, its Swedish name, how it sounds and an example word.

Å, Ä and Ö are letters in their own right, not an A or O with an accent. In a Swedish dictionary they come after Z, and swapping them for a plain a or o changes the word.

How to Type Å, Ä and Ö

Phone: press and hold A or O and pick the letter you need.

Mac: Option+A gives å. For ä and ö, press Option+U and then a or o.

Windows: hold Alt and type on the number pad: 0229 for å, 0228 for ä, 0246 for ö (capitals: 0197 for Å, 0196 for Ä, 0214 for Ö). You can also add the Swedish keyboard layout in your language settings.

💡 Long and short vowels: every Swedish vowel can be long or short. As a rule of thumb, a vowel is long before a single consonant (glas, glass) and short before a double consonant (glass, ice cream).
