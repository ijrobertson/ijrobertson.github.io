O Alfabeto PortuguêsThe Portuguese Alphabet

📚 Explore Portuguese: Portuguese Basics Accent Marks First Conversation At the Restaurant Typical Day Portuguese Hub →

The Portuguese alphabet has 26 letters, the same as English. The letters K, W and Y were officially added in the 1990 spelling agreement and appear only in foreign words and names. The table below gives each letter, its Portuguese name, how it sounds and an example word.

Portuguese also writes five marks on its letters, which creates 12 accented letters. They are listed further down, and our guide to Portuguese accent marks explains each one in detail.

Portuguese Letters with Accents

These 12 accented letters are not separate letters of the alphabet. They are ordinary letters carrying a mark that changes the stress or the sound.

💡 Letter pairs to know: Portuguese has several two-letter combinations with their own sound: ch ("sh" as in chave, key), lh ("lli" in million, as in filho, son), nh ("ny" in canyon, as in vinho, wine), rr and ss.
